Privacy Policy — WavePublish

Last updated: 30 July 2026

WavePublish ("the App") is a personal macOS desktop application used by its operator to publish their own short-form video content to their own social media accounts, including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Who operates the App

The App is an independent software project operated by a single person in Türkiye, for their own private use. It is not a company product and is not offered to third parties. Contact: etemmkocaa@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data from third parties. It only processes:

  • Video files that the operator explicitly selects to upload;
  • Captions/descriptions the operator writes for those posts;
  • Access tokens for the operator's own connected accounts (e.g. YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok), used solely to publish on the operator's behalf;
  • Public statistics (such as view and like counts) for the operator's own posts, retrieved from those same accounts and stored locally.

3. Multiple content brands

The App may be used for more than one of the operator's own content brands. Each brand's credentials and settings are stored separately on the operator's computer, and a publish only ever uses the credentials belonging to the brand selected for that post. Data is not combined or shared between brands.

4. Where data is stored

Access tokens and configuration are stored locally on the operator's own computer. They are not transmitted to any third-party server operated by us. Videos are temporarily made available over a private, expiring link only so that the destination platform can fetch the file during upload; the link is closed immediately after the upload completes.

5. Third-party platforms

When the operator publishes content, the App communicates directly with the official APIs of the chosen platforms (such as YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok). Use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Data sharing

We do not share any data with advertisers or other third parties. The App has no analytics, tracking, or advertising components.

7. Data retention & deletion

Because all data is stored locally, there is no server-side account to delete. The operator can remove data at any time by:

  • deleting the App's files and its local configuration folder from their computer, which removes stored tokens, publish history and cached statistics; and
  • revoking the App's access from each platform's own settings (for example, the "Apps and websites" or "Connected apps" section of the relevant account), which invalidates any token previously issued to the App.

Anyone wishing to confirm deletion, or to request it, can write to etemmkocaa@gmail.com and it will be handled directly by the operator.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process data from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: etemmkocaa@gmail.com.
