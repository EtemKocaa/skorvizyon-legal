Privacy Policy — Skor Vizyon

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Skor Vizyon ("the App") is a personal desktop application used by its owner to publish their own football (soccer) short-video content to their own social media accounts, including TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated privately by its individual owner for personal use. Contact: etemmkocaa@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data from third parties. It only processes:

  • Video files that the owner explicitly selects to upload;
  • Captions/descriptions the owner writes for those posts;
  • Access tokens for the owner's own connected accounts (e.g. TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Facebook), used solely to publish on the owner's behalf.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens and configuration are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not transmitted to any third-party server operated by us. Videos are temporarily made available over a private, expiring link only so that the destination platform (e.g. TikTok) can fetch the file during upload; the link is closed immediately after the upload completes.

4. Third-party platforms

When the owner publishes content, the App communicates directly with the official APIs of the chosen platforms (such as TikTok, Instagram, Threads and Facebook). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

We do not share any data with advertisers or other third parties. The App has no analytics, tracking, or advertising components.

6. Data retention & deletion

Because data is stored locally, the owner can delete it at any time by removing the App's files from their computer or revoking access tokens from the respective platform's settings.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: etemmkocaa@gmail.com.
